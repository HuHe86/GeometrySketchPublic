## 2) General Information About the App

GeometrySketch is a drawing and geometry app. Drawings, documents, and settings are stored by the app locally on your device or in storage locations you choose.

GeometrySketch does not require a separate user account with the app provider. The app does not automatically transmit drawings or documents to the provider's own servers. The provider does not operate a server-side service for synchronizing this content or verifying store purchases.

The app does not contain its own advertising, tracking, or analytics features that transmit your usage behavior to the provider.

This should be distinguished from:

- communication with the respective store to process purchases and verify entitlements;
- storage, backup, and cloud services you select;
- exports, sharing, and support requests you initiate;
- independent processing by operating system and store providers.

## 3) Locally Stored Data

Depending on how you use the app, it stores or processes, in particular:

- drawings, documents, and any text or images they contain;
- app settings, such as language and display preferences;
- information about selected files and storage locations;
- generated export files, such as PDF and image files;
- purchase and license information used to unlock paid features.

Documents may contain personal data if you enter or import such content. The app processes this content to provide the features you use.

The following purchase and license information may be stored locally:

- the store used;
- whether a subscription is active;
- the product identifier;
- a purchase or transaction identifier, if provided by the store;
- an expiration date, if available;
- the time of the most recent status check.

If you import a school license, its contents are also stored locally in the app settings. The license file may contain, among other things, a name, an email address, an expiration date, and a digital signature.

Local processing serves to operate the app and provide or unlock its features. Where personal data is necessary to perform the agreement governing your use of the app, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 4) File Access, Export, Sharing, and Clipboard

The app may use system features to open, import, save, export, and share files, as well as to access the clipboard.

When you share content, copy it to the clipboard, or save it to an external storage location, that content may become accessible to the apps or services you select, or to other applications authorized under your system settings.

In particular, saving files in a synchronized folder or with a cloud storage provider may cause those files to be transmitted to that provider's servers. Operating system backups may also include app data. Such transfers take place through the respective service, not through a cloud service operated by GeometrySketch.

Any further processing is governed by the privacy notices of the relevant providers.

## 5) In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Depending on the platform and availability, paid features may be purchased or subscribed to through the respective store:

- **Google Play** on Android;
- **Apple App Store** on iOS and macOS;
- **Microsoft Store** on Windows.

The app uses the respective platform provider's purchase and licensing interfaces for this purpose. These interfaces are also used to restore purchases and verify existing entitlements. A status check may take place when the app starts or when you initiate a refresh, not only when you make a new purchase.

The respective store and its payment service providers may process, in particular, account, purchase, payment, device, and technical connection data. The specific data processed depends on the store and the services it uses.

GeometrySketch receives the purchase and license information necessary to unlock the relevant features. Payment details such as complete credit card numbers or login credentials for your store account are not made available to the app.

The app does not transmit this purchase and license information to the provider's own servers for verification.

The provider may receive sales, settlement, and statistical reports through the stores' administrative interfaces. The scope of this information and whether it includes personal data depend on the respective store. Such reports are not equivalent to GeometrySketch independently recording your drawing activity or app usage behavior.

The following privacy policies apply, in particular, to the platform providers' independent processing:

- **Google:** https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
- **Apple:** https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/
- **Microsoft:** https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement

Store subscriptions are managed and canceled through the respective platform provider. Deleting the app or its local data does not automatically cancel a subscription.

## 6) School Licenses

School licenses are requested and purchased directly from the provider, outside the in-app purchase systems.

The following information is processed, in particular, to handle inquiries, prepare offers, issue invoices, and provide the license:

- the contact person's name;
- the school's or contact person's email address;
- the school's name and address;
- the total number of students, to determine the license price;
- invoice, payment, and correspondence data.

Only the total number of students is required. Lists of students' names or individual student usage data are not required to issue a school license.

Processing takes place to enter into and perform the license agreement and to comply with legal obligations. Where applicable, the legal bases are Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. The processing of contact persons' professional contact details may also be based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is communicating with the school and administering the agreement.

Email communication and payments are handled through the email and banking services used for those purposes. These services receive the data necessary to provide their respective services.

After import, the supplied license file is verified locally on the device using its digital signature and any expiration date it contains. Its contents are not transmitted to the provider's own license server for this verification.

If a school distributes the license file to authorized individuals, those individuals may be able to access the information it contains. The file should therefore only be shared within the intended group of authorized recipients.

## 7) Support and Contact

If you contact the provider by email, your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you submit are processed to handle your request.

Contacting the provider is voluntary. Please send only the information necessary for your request and avoid unnecessary personal data, particularly confidential student data.

Depending on the nature of your request, processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is handling inquiries and assisting you with using the app.

## 8) Statistical Information and Diagnostic Data

Operating system and store providers may process information about installations, devices, use of their platforms, and technical errors as part of their own services. This depends, among other things, on the platform, system settings, and the respective privacy options.

They may provide the app provider with statistical analyses or technical error reports. The app itself does not contain its own service for automatically transmitting drawings or documents to the provider as diagnostic data.

Data collection by platform providers is governed by their respective privacy notices.

## 9) External Links and Services

The app may link to external websites or applications, such as GitHub, store pages, or social networks.

When you open such a link, the respective operator may process your IP address, browser or device information, and, where applicable, account information. The respective operator is responsible for this processing.

Depending on the provider, processing may take place outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Information about recipients, processing locations, and any safeguards used for international transfers can be found in the privacy notice of the relevant service.

## 10) Retention and Deletion

Locally stored documents and export files generally remain stored until you delete them or they are removed by the respective storage service. App settings and locally stored license information may be changed or removed through updates, removal of a license, or deletion of the app's data.

Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove all files. Exports you have saved, files in external folders, and backup copies may remain and may need to be deleted separately.

Contact and support data is retained only for as long as necessary to handle the request and, where applicable, protect legitimate interests.

Contract, invoice, and payment records are retained for the applicable statutory retention periods. They are subsequently deleted unless another legal ground requires their retention.

Data held by stores, cloud providers, backup services, and payment services is subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

## 11) Your Rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have, in particular, the following rights against the respective controller:

- access to your personal data;
- rectification of inaccurate data;
- erasure;
- restriction of processing;
- data portability;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interests.

You may withdraw any consent you have given for the processing of personal data at any time, with effect for the future.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Requests concerning data processing by the provider of GeometrySketch can be sent to geometrysketch@outlook.de. For data independently processed by a store or another external service, please contact the respective operator.

The provider has no direct access to app content stored exclusively on your device.

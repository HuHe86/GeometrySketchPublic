# One-Year School License for GeometrySketch

*Last updated: 7 October 2026*

## 1) Scope of the School License

A one-year school license entitles students and teachers of the licensed school to use the paid features of GeometrySketch included in the respective offer throughout the agreed license period.

The price is based on the school's total number of students, not on the number of devices used or active users.

The license applies exclusively to the school identified in the offer or invoice. Other schools or institutions are not automatically included.

The specific offer and the terms agreed upon at the time of purchase determine the scope of services. There is no entitlement to specific future features unless they have been expressly promised.

## 2) Supported Devices and Installation

The school license can be used on compatible devices on which GeometrySketch is available and importing a school license is supported.

Please check compatibility with the intended devices and operating systems before purchasing. Technical requirements are set out in the respective store description.

The Application and its updates must generally be obtained from the designated official store:

- Google Play on Android;
- Apple App Store on iOS and macOS;
- Microsoft Store on Windows.

Availability may vary by platform, region, and device. The school license does not guarantee that the Application will run on every device or operating system version.

An internet connection and an account with the respective store may be required for downloads and updates.

## 3) License Period and Renewal

The school license has a fixed term of one year.

The start of the license period and the specific expiration date are agreed upon at the time of purchase or communicated when the license is provided. The expiration date is recorded in the license file.

The license does not renew automatically. No cancellation is therefore required at the end of the agreed period.

To continue using the licensed features afterward, a new license or an expressly agreed renewal is required.

Once the license expires, it no longer grants access to the paid features that depend on it. Any separate, valid store entitlement remains unaffected.

## 4) Prices

The price of a one-year school license is based on the licensed school's total number of students:

The number of teachers, active users, and devices used does not affect this pricing structure.

The prices and tax information stated in the offer apply to the specific purchase.

## 6) Activation Using the License File

The license file must be imported into GeometrySketch on the authorized devices. It can be imported through the license prompt displayed when the Application starts or later through the **“About This App”** menu item.

The Application verifies the license file's digital signature and expiration date locally on the device. The file's contents are not transmitted to the provider's own license server for this verification.

The imported license is stored locally in the app settings. You may need to import it again after deleting app data, reinstalling the Application, or changing devices.

Please therefore retain a backup copy of the license file.

## 7) Distribution Within the School

The school may distribute the license file to its authorized students and teachers to the extent necessary for use within the agreed license scope.

The following are not permitted:

- publishing the license file publicly;
- sharing it with individuals outside the authorized group;
- using it to activate the Application for other schools or institutions;
- modifying or tampering with the license file.

The license file may contain information about the school or license holder, particularly a name, an email address, and the expiration date. Recipients of the file may be able to access this information.

Please therefore distribute the file only through appropriate channels within the authorized group.

## 8) Relationship to Store Subscriptions

The school license is purchased directly from the provider and is not a subscription with Google Play, Apple, or Microsoft.

No additional store subscription is required to use the features covered by a valid school license.

Purchasing or importing a school license does not automatically cancel an existing store subscription. If necessary, that subscription must be canceled separately through the respective store.

The conditions for managing, canceling, and obtaining refunds for a store subscription are governed by the respective purchase agreement, the store's terms, and applicable law.

## 9) Privacy

The total number of students is used to determine the license price. Individual usage data or lists of students' names are not required for the purchase.

Further information about the processing of contact, invoice, and license data, as well as local storage, can be found in the **Privacy Notice for GeometrySketch**.

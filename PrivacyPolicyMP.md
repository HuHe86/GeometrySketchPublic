# Privacy Policy – GeometrySketch
**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GeometrySketch handles data. GeometrySketch is designed to operate **without collecting personal data** on its own servers.

## 1) Personal Data
GeometrySketch does **not** collect, store, share, or process personal data on any servers controlled by the app developer.

## 2) Local Data Storage
To function properly, the app stores data **locally on your device**, including:

- Drawings and documents  
- App settings  
- Exported files (e.g., PDF or images) that you create manually  

These files remain on your device or in the storage location you choose unless you delete or share them yourself.

## 3) File Access, Sharing, and Clipboard
GeometrySketch may use system functions **only when you initiate an action**, such as:

- Opening or saving files  
- Exporting or sharing content  
- Copying content to the clipboard  

Any data transfer occurs solely to apps or destinations you explicitly select.

## 4) In‑App Purchases
If you use in‑app purchases, payment processing is handled by the respective platform provider (e.g., Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, Google Play).  
These providers may process personal data as part of the transaction.

## 5) Statistics and Diagnostic Data
Depending on the platform, the operating system or store provider (e.g., Microsoft) may collect statistical usage data, device information, or diagnostic reports.

Further information:  
https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement

## 6) External Links
The app may open external websites (e.g., support or documentation pages).  
The operators of those websites are responsible for their own data processing.

## 7) Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.  
Updates take effect once published. You are encouraged to review this page periodically.

## 8) Contact
If you have any questions about privacy or data protection, please contact the app provider.

# 1-Year School License for GeometrySketch

*Updated on 10/06/2026*

## 1) Terms
By purchasing a 1-year school license, all members of the school community—students and teachers—are entitled to use the application fully until the subscription expires. However, this can only apply to devices whose compatibility **you** have previously checked. The application and its updates must generally be downloaded from the respective stores.

## 2) Prices
The price of the 1-year school license for GeometrySketch depends on the total number of students—not the actual number of users—at the school. For fewer than 500 students, the license costs €200 per year; otherwise, €300.

The subscription automatically expires after one year and does not need to be canceled separately.

## 3) Purchase 
To acquire a school license, you need to send an email to geometrysketch@outlook.de with the following information: - School email address - Contact person's name - Name and address of the school - Number of students You will then receive an invoice, which should be paid via bank transfer, referencing the invoice number. After your payment is received, you will get a license file by email that allows you to unlock all the app's features on the authorized devices. The file needs to be loaded either at program startup or later in the 'About this App' menu.
